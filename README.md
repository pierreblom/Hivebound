# Hivebound

Hivebound is an original, touch-friendly bee-colony engine-builder prototype. Arrange a living hex hive, automate nectar into honey, manage the colony climate, send risky foraging expeditions, trade on a shifting market, meet annual quotas, and stack permanent Royal Crests.

Version 0.2 adds four changing seasons, four distinct biomes, three difficulty modes, emergency climate responses, individual egg/larva/pupa development, adjustable foraging crews, market standing orders, detailed year reports, paid Crest rerolls, a Crest Archive, and persistent accessibility/settings controls. The interface is now a mobile-first installable web app with phone-safe portrait and landscape layouts, large touch targets, safe-area support, an offline shell, and a compact market desk for small screens.

## Play locally

```bash
npm start
```

Open [http://localhost:4173](http://localhost:4173).

No package installation is required. The game uses only browser-native HTML, CSS, SVG, and JavaScript.

## How to play

1. Choose a biome and difficulty, then pick a cell from the build bar and place it on a glowing hex beside the hive.
2. Start with a **Nectar Garden**, **Amber Refinery**, and **Honey Vault**.
3. Deliver honey to the Crown before the year timer expires.
4. Sell surplus honey when the Amber Exchange price is high.
5. Send spare workers foraging and adjust the crew size to trade labour for speed and yield.
6. Respond to seasonal climate changes with vents or short emergency actions.
7. Open Brood cells to follow development and feed honey to hasten a worker's birth.
8. Add Sentinel Posts before raids begin in Year 3.
9. Complete each year to review the colony, reroll choices if needed, and claim a permanent Royal Crest.

Progress saves automatically in the browser.

On a phone, use **Add to Home Screen** (iOS) or **Install app** (Android/Chrome) to launch Hivebound like a standalone mobile game. Portrait and landscape are both supported.
